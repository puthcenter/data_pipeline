**Data Availability Notice**
The algorithm design and parameter calibration are informed by real-world data-flow graph characteristics from Alibaba's production environment. 
**The original production data cannot be disclosed due to trade secret protection.** A single de-identified real-world sample (`dag121037_MEDIUM_0`, ~109K nodes, ~564K edges) is provided in `data/output/` for reference. 
Additional samples may be added in the future. For full-scale benchmarking, use the synthetic data generator `data/pipeline_set.py`.
